Refreshes every 15 minutes, or on Refresh now. Reads the pool's resting orders straight from Sui mainnet (graphql.mainnet.sui.io), pool . Expired orders are left out, so the levels match the indexer's /orderbook/HBTC_USDC. Mid = (best bid + best ask) / 2. USD = Σ price × remaining size over every order priced within 1% of mid (bids ≥ mid × 0.99, asks ≤ mid × 1.01), with USDC counted as $1. Known makers are listed even when they have nothing on the book. Any other address with at least $1,000 inside the band gets its own row marked New. Smaller ones are grouped under Other addresses.
